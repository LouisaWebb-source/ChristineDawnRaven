3. Age confirmation and browser storage

The homepage asks you to confirm that you are at least 18. When you select “I am 18+ — Enter”, it stores cdr18=yes in your browser’s sessionStorage. This remembers your choice for the current tab’s session so the prompt does not reappear on each visit in that session. It does not collect your date of birth or verify your identity, and the code does not transmit this value to us or a third party.

The value normally expires when the tab/window session ends, although browser session restoration can preserve it. You can remove it by clearing this website’s browser storage. This limited storage supports the age-restricted access you request when entering the website; we rely on § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG for this necessary function. To the extent personal data is processed, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in remembering the access confirmation for mature content.

The website code sets no cookies and uses no localStorage. Impressum and Privacy remain available without entering the age-restricted homepage.

4. External book and social links

The site contains ordinary links to GoodNovel, Inkitt, Amazon, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. It does not embed their widgets, tracking pixels or media. These providers are not contacted by the site code merely because a link is displayed. When you follow a link, the destination receives your request and processes data under its own privacy policy. These links use rel="noopener noreferrer" to prevent sending the referring page address.

The age prompt’s “Leave” button redirects to Google. Google receives the resulting request under its own privacy policy; the button does not load Google content before you select it.

6a. GoatCounter visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to measure page visits, book/social-link clicks and views of homepage sections. These statistics help us understand reader interest and improve the website. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in proportionate audience measurement.

GoatCounter receives technical request data and produces aggregate counts for pages/events, referrers, browsers, operating systems, screen sizes and countries. Individual pageview records, regions and language collection are disabled. IP addresses and browser headers are processed temporarily for country detection and recognising repeat visits for up to eight hours, rather than stored in the statistics database. The analytics uses no cookies or browser storage and does not track visitors across websites.

The service is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland, with hosting at Hetzner in Germany and Finland. Our dashboard is private, and statistics are retained for 365 days. See the GoatCounter privacy policy. You can object through the contact above or block requests to gc.zgo.at and christinedawnraven.goatcounter.com in your browser. Book/social links continue to work if analytics is blocked.
